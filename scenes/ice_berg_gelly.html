How to play

Calve the glacier. Throw fish. Sound the horn.

Slabs break off the ice cliff every minute or so, fall in with a splash and roll over until they float the way they like · Cast off sends Polaris round the bay, shouldering the pack aside (press again for full ahead) · thrown fish bring the penguins racing down from the rookery; they keep clear of the orca · Horn (or Space) · pick up a floe or a berg and throw it · drag across the water to make waves · drag the ground or the side of the block to tip it · drag empty space or right-drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom
